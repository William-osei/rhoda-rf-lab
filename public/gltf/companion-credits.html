Detailed male lab companion

Medical_Male_01 and the native male breathe, neutral walk and wave animations are from the Microsoft Rocketbox library. The library's avatar and animation assets are released under the MIT license, copyright Microsoft 2020. The complete required notice accompanies this model.

The official repository credits Markus Wojcik and the original Rocketbox avatar team, plus Matias Volonte and Fang Ma for the facial blendshapes. Its research reference is The Rocketbox library and the utility of freely available rigged avatars.

RHODA converts the original FBX to glTF, uses indexed geometry and compact textures, and retains 52 ARKit facial targets and 15 visemes. The figure is normalized to 1.68 metres. Native body rotation clips preserve its own joint attachments; navigation controls world movement.

Blinking, a quiet gaze shift and an illustrative speech-state mouth envelope are enabled with gentle animation. Browser read-aloud provides speaking state, not phoneme timestamps. This is not phonetic lip synchronization, live facial tracking or an exact replica of the reference image.

Lightweight fallback companion

Male 10 by Elbolilloduro, from Characters PSX, is released under CC0 1.0. Its original textures are preserved.

Human rig adaptation and the CC0 Idle_Subtle, Greeting and Walk animations are supplied by Mesh2Motion. This independent character is used if the detailed model cannot load. It has no facial morph targets.
